# Company Sales Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the company's sales performance to gain valuable insights into revenue, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, and sales trends over time.

The analysis was conducted using an interactive dashboard to transform sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

By examining different aspects of the company's performance, this project aims to identify profitable areas, highlight underperforming products, understand customer purchasing behavior, and provide recommendations to improve overall business performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate the company's overall sales and profitability performance.
- Identify the regions generating the highest sales and profits.
- Analyze revenue contributions across different customer segments.
- Determine the best-performing product categories.
- Identify the most profitable and loss-generating products.
- Analyze annual and monthly sales trends.
- Identify opportunities for improving business performance.
- Provide actionable recommendations to support management decisions.

## 📋 Detailed Business Analysis

### 1. What is the overall sales performance of the company?

The business achieved solid top-line performance with €2.30M in total revenue and €2.01M in total operational costs. Net profit stands at €286.40K (12.47% margin) across 9,994 total orders, resulting in an average sales of €229.86.

### 2. Which region generates the highest sales and profits?

According to our region be sales and profits charts on the dashboard

- **West:** generated the highest sales at €0.73M and the highest profits at €0.11M
- **East:** Follows closely with €0.68M in sales and €0.09M in profit
- **Central:** Generates €0.50M in sales and recorded the lowest profit of €0.04M
- **South:** Records the lowest figures with €0.39M in sales and second to the lowest in profit of €0.05M

### 3. Which customer segments contributes the most revenue?

According to the Customer Segment By Revenue chart on the dashboard we can see that the consumer Contributed the highest revenue at 50.56% (€1.16M) of the total sales, followed by corporate, which represented the second-largest share at 30.74% (€0.71M), while home office Accounted for the remaining 18.70% (€0.43M) of total revenue.

### 4. Which product categories perform best?

According to the Products Categories By Sales And Profits chart on the dashboard

- **Technology (best performer):** Leads all categories with €0.84M in total sales and €0.15M in net profit, making it both the highest revenue generator and the most profitable category
- **Furniture :** Generates solid top-line revenue at €0.74M, but yields a very low net profit of only €0.02M, heavily impacted by unprofitable sub-categories like tables and bookcases.
- **Office supplies:** Generates strong and highly efficient returns with €0.72M in sales and €0.12M in profit

### 5. Which products are the most profitable?

According to the Top 5 Most Profitable Products chart on the dashboard, the highest-earning items by net profit are:

1. Canon imageCLASS 2200 advanced copier: €25K in profit
2. Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch plastic comb Binding Machine with manual blind: €8K in profit
3. Hewlett Packard LaserJet 3310 Copier: €7K in profit
4. Canon PC1060 Personal laser Copier: €5K in profit
5. HP Designjet T520 Inkjet large format Printer: €4K in profit

Additionally I also highlighted 5 items generating the greatest financial losses which are:

1. Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head: Approximately -€8.9K in loss.
2. Lexmark MX611dhe Monochrome Laser Printer: Approximately -€4.6K in loss.
3. Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Triple head print: Approximately -€3.8K in loss.
4. Chromcraft Bull-Nose Wood oval conference tables & bases: Approximately -€2.9K in loss.
5. Bush Advantage Collection Racetrack Conference Table: Approximately -€1.9K in loss.

### 6. What trend can be observed overtime?

Based on the Annual Sales Trends and Monthly Sales Performance charts on the dashboard, we can see that Total annual revenue experienced steady expansion over the multi-year period, rising from €0.48M in 2014 and €0.47M in 2015 to €0.61M in 2016, and reaching a peak of €0.73M in 2017. Then on monthly sales performance chart, Sales begin at their lowest points in January (€0.09M) and February (€0.06M), then Activity picks up moderately in March (€0.21M) before settling into a steady baseline ranging between €0.14M and €0.16M per month from April through August, then Momentum builds starting in late summer, with August (€0.16M) showing steady growth to september(€0.31M), then droped a bit in October (€0.20M) and peaks at its highest point in November (€0.35M), remaining exceptionally strong through December (€0.33M) due to year-end shopping and business purchasing trends.

### 7. What recommendations should management implement to improve business performance?

Based on the performance metrics across the dashboard, I recommend management should

1. Restructure or Discontinue Unprofitable Products, like the Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head (-€8.9K loss) and the Bush Advantage Collection Racetrack conference table(-€1.9K loss).
2. Focus Resources on High-Margin Categories by Prioritizing marketing and inventory allocation toward high-performing product lines such as Technology, which delivers the highest overall revenue (€0.84M) and net profit (€0.15M),and also capitalize on exceptionally profitable sub-categories like Copiers, which yield €55.62K in profit on €149.53K in sales.
3. Capitalize on Q4 Seasonal Surges by Preparing inventory, staffing, and marketing campaigns well ahead of the heavy Q4 buying season, aligning with historical trends where monthly revenue peaks significantly between November and December (reaching up to €0.35M in November)

## 🚀 Business Recommendations Summary

Based on the analysis, management should consider:

- Reviewing products that consistently generate financial losses.
- Increasing investment in high-performing product categories.
- Strengthening marketing strategies for the consumer customer segment.
- Maintaining strong regional sales performance while identifying growth opportunities in other regions.
- Preparing inventory and resources ahead of the fourth-quarter sales peak.
